Plumbing, PHE & Water Management

Water supply, sanitation, drainage, treatment and pumping systems, from coordinated delivery to everyday facility operations.

01

PHE engineering & system delivery

  • Hot/cold water distribution, insulation, pressure zoning and sanitary-fixture installation.
  • Soil and waste stacks, vents, traps and gravity-drainage design coordination.
  • External drainage networks, chambers and civic-mains connection coordination.
  • Roof drainage, surface run-off and recharge provisions.
  • WTP/STP packages, booster sets, sumps, control panels and redundancy arrangements, with installation, testing and commissioning where contracted.
02

Water supply & storage

  • Operation and inspection of domestic cold/hot water supply, transfer pumps and hydro-pneumatic systems.
  • Inspection of overhead and underground tanks, water storage arrangements and water heaters.
  • Monitoring of water levels, pressure, pump operation and leakage.
  • Checks of valves, strainers, non-return valves, pressure gauges and pipe connections.
  • Coordination of scheduled water-tank cleaning and water-quality testing.
03

Sanitation & drainage

  • Leak attendance for water supply, drainage and plumbing lines.
  • Maintenance of sinks, toilets, faucets, flush valves, showers, sensor taps and sanitary fittings.
  • Clearing minor blockages in traps, floor drains and accessible drainage lines.
  • Inspection of sewage pumps, sump pumps and drainage pumping systems.
  • Rainwater drainage, stormwater pumps, accessible manholes, greywater systems and rainwater harvesting structures.
04

Treatment & monitoring

  • STP, ETP, filtration and water-treatment system operation, monitoring and maintenance where contracted.
  • Water-quality testing coordination for treatment and recycling units.
  • Water consumption records, pump logs, leakage reports and preventive maintenance records.
SUPPORTING RESOURCES

Equipment, tools & support

Equipment covered and resources planned for the agreed work may include:

  • Equipment: transfer pumps, submersible sewage pumps, filtration units, water heaters and hydro-pneumatic tanks.
  • Tools: pipe and basin wrenches, PVC/PPR fusion equipment, pipe cutters and pipe vices.
  • Instruments: hydraulic pressure-test pumps and drain inspection cameras.
  • Spares: PVC, CPVC, PPR and GI pipes, elbows, tees, reducers, sockets, end caps, ball/check/butterfly/pressure-reducing valves, faucet cartridges, flush valves and sensor taps.
  • Repair consumables: PTFE tape, solvent cement, silicone sealants, rubber gaskets and pipe clamps.

Scope & service coordination

STP/ETP and pump maintenance, specialist treatment work and associated consumables are subject to a separately agreed scope and charges where applicable.

Supply of materials, tools, spares and specialist services is confirmed in the contract. Equipment listed here describes service coverage and planned resources.
