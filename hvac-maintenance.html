01

System delivery & annual contracts

  • Supply, installation, testing and commissioning of central chilled-water, VRF and packaged air-conditioning systems within the agreed project scope.
  • Precision air-conditioning for server rooms, control rooms and process areas.
  • Ventilation and smoke-exhaust design coordination, installation and approval liaison where contracted.
  • Annual maintenance contracts (AMC) and comprehensive annual maintenance contracts (CAMC), with system coverage and exclusions agreed in writing.
02

Cooling & ventilation

  • Operation and monitoring of chillers, AHUs, FCUs, VRF/VRV systems and packaged air-conditioning units.
  • Cooling tower inspection, basin cleaning, water-level checks and performance monitoring.
  • Basement ventilation, smoke extraction, toilet exhaust fans and kitchen hood systems.
  • Air-filter cleaning and replacement, duct cleaning and fresh-air optimisation for indoor air quality.
03

Mechanical inspections & repairs

  • Inspection of motors, compressors, pumps, chilled-water pipes, valves, strainers, insulation and flexible connections.
  • Refrigerant leak checks and monitoring of system pressures, temperatures and operating performance.
  • Lubrication of bearings and moving parts; checks of belts, pulleys, couplings, vibration and abnormal noise.
  • Breakdown attendance, fault diagnosis, minor repairs and approved component replacement.
04

Controls & operating records

  • Inspection of thermostats, sensors, actuators and HVAC control panels.
  • Monitoring of BMS alarms, temperature settings and equipment operating schedules.
  • HVAC logs, equipment history, energy readings and preventive maintenance checklists.
05

Specialist utility systems

  • Coordination of maintenance for compressed-air, steam and process cooling-water systems where installed and contracted.
  • Specialist coordination for PNG, LPG and HSD fuel piping within the agreed site scope.
SUPPORTING RESOURCES

Equipment, tools & support

Equipment covered and resources planned for the agreed work may include:

  • Equipment: chillers, AHUs, FCUs, VRF units, ventilation fans, pumps and motors.
  • Tools: pipe benders, duct cutters, hydraulic crimpers, welding and pipe-threading equipment, as required for approved work.
  • Instruments: anemometers, digital manometers, leak detectors and temperature/humidity data loggers.
  • Spares: air and HEPA filters, V-belts, bearings, thermostats, specified refrigerants, insulation, condensate pipes, seals, gland packing, couplings, vibration pads and alignment shims.

Scope & service coordination

Specialist repairs, refrigerant work, utility piping and pump maintenance are defined and priced in the agreed scope; OEM or specialist attendance is coordinated where required.

Supply of materials, tools, spares and specialist services is confirmed in the contract. Equipment listed here describes service coverage and planned resources.
