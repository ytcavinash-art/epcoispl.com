Electrical & Power Systems

Power distribution, backup supplies, lighting and electrical protection, from engineering and installation through testing and maintenance.

01

Electrical engineering & installation

  • Substation layout, civil interfaces, utility liaison and energisation coordination.
  • HT/LT switchgear selection, protection coordination, settings and cable-sizing support.
  • Transformer and diesel-generator sizing, redundancy, synchronisation and load-transfer planning.
  • MCC, PCC and automatic power factor correction (APFC) panels, motor controls and metering.
  • Lighting design, small power, containment, equipotential bonding and surge protection, with installation and testing under the agreed package.
02

Power distribution

  • Operation and monitoring of HT/LT panels, transformers, RMUs, PCCs, MCCs, main distribution boards, sub-panels and busways/bus ducts.
  • Inspection for overheating, abnormal noise, damage, cable termination issues, busbar condition and panel indications.
  • Recording of voltage, current, frequency, power factor and energy consumption.
  • Scheduled thermography and electrical testing under the approved maintenance plan.
03

Backup & critical power

  • Inspection, operation and testing coordination for diesel generators, AMF/ATS panels, batteries and auxiliaries.
  • Monitoring and maintenance coordination for UPS systems, inverters, battery banks and critical power supplies.
  • Response to power failures, nuisance tripping, electrical faults and emergency breakdowns.
  • Solar power system monitoring and specialist maintenance coordination where included in the contract.
04

Lighting & electrical safety

  • Internal, external and emergency lighting maintenance, including energy-efficient lighting controls.
  • Replacement of defective lamps, LED drivers, switches, sockets, MCBs and approved minor accessories.
  • Inspection of earthing connections and scheduled resistance testing of grounding systems.
  • Lightning protection system inspections and scheduled testing.
  • Electrical logs, meter readings, maintenance records and test reports.
SUPPORTING RESOURCES

Equipment, tools & support

Equipment covered and resources planned for the agreed work may include:

  • Equipment: transformers, RMUs, diesel generators, distribution boards and UPS systems.
  • Tools: cable-pulling winches, wire strippers, hydraulic lug crimpers, conduit benders and knockout punches.
  • Instruments: multimeters, clamp meters, insulation testers, earth resistance testers, lux meters and phase rotation meters.
  • Spares: cable lugs, ferrules, glands, insulation tape, heat-shrink sleeves, terminals, cable ties, MCBs, MCCBs, contactors, relays, fuses, indicator lamps, LED lamps/drivers, flexible wires, switches, sockets and junction boxes.
  • Safety equipment: insulated mats and task-appropriate electrical and arc-flash protection.

Scope & service coordination

Solar maintenance, OEM specialist services, major spares and capital replacements are separately scoped. Switching, isolation and testing follow approved site procedures.

Supply of materials, tools, spares and specialist services is confirmed in the contract. Equipment listed here describes service coverage and planned resources.
