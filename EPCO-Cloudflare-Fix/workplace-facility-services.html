01

Soft FM & workplace operations

  • Daily, periodic and deep housekeeping cycles planned by area type.
  • Waste segregation, storage and authorised disposal coordination.
  • Mechanised cleaning, including scrubbing, facade cleaning and high-level access through appropriately equipped service teams.
  • Washroom consumables, sanitisation and pest-control coordination.
  • Manned guarding coordination, access records and patrol logs.
  • Horticulture, landscape care, reception and front-desk support.
02

Workplace services

  • Coordination of housekeeping, security services, landscaping and facility utilities.
  • Space utilisation planning and workplace improvement initiatives.
  • Facility improvement projects that support efficiency, sustainability and occupant satisfaction.
03

Vendor & commercial administration

  • Facilities-related vendor, contractor and service-provider coordination.
  • Facility budget monitoring, operating-cost review and resource utilisation tracking.
  • OEM attendance, specialist contractor management, warranty claims and outsourced testing coordination.
04

Safety & operational continuity

  • Coordination of health, safety, environmental and facility compliance activities.
  • Support for business continuity, emergency preparedness and operational risk management.
  • Permit-to-work, lockout/tagout, PPE, electrical isolation and safe-work procedure coordination.
  • Facility records, maintenance schedules, asset inventories and workplace service reports.
SUPPORTING RESOURCES

Equipment, tools & support

Equipment covered and resources planned for the agreed work may include:

  • Site-specific staffing may include facility/operations managers, discipline leads, helpdesk, MIS/admin support, EHS and energy-monitoring roles.
  • Housekeeping, security, landscaping and specialist services are coordinated through the agreed delivery model.

Scope & service coordination

Manpower, service hours, vendor responsibilities and workplace service inclusions are specified for each facility. Staffing is based on the site's needs and agreed service levels.
