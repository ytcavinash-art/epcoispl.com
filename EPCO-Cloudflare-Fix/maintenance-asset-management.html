01

Lifecycle contracts & service governance

  • Preventive, corrective and breakdown maintenance across civil/building fabric, MEP, ELV and utility systems within the agreed asset boundary.
  • AMC/CAMC coverage, asset warranty status, vendor attendance and contract renewal tracking.
  • Asset replacement forecasts and capital-budget planning support.
  • Response, resolution, availability, PPM completion and downtime KPIs reviewed against agreed targets.
  • Monthly owner reviews, transparent consumables/vendor-cost reporting and documented handovers when site teams change, where included in the contract.
02

Planned & corrective maintenance

  • Daily equipment rounds, meter readings, alarm monitoring and operating-status checks.
  • Preventive maintenance schedules covering daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and annual tasks as applicable.
  • Scheduled servicing, calibration and inspections for maintainable assets.
  • Fault diagnosis, minor repairs, approved consumable replacement and restoration of services.
  • Breakdown complaint registration, prioritisation, response, escalation and closure; round-the-clock shift coverage where contracted.
03

Helpdesk, assets & performance

  • Helpdesk and computerised maintenance management system (CMMS) work-order logging.
  • Tracking of response/restoration SLAs, maintenance history, equipment health and recurring faults.
  • Asset lifecycle planning, retrofit recommendations and equipment-replacement planning.
  • Electricity, fuel and water consumption monitoring and equipment efficiency review.
  • Coordination of applicable inspections, tests, licences, renewals and statutory records.
04

Stores & material handling

  • Critical-spares and consumables inventory, minimum stock levels and procurement follow-up.
  • Materials/stock loading, unloading and handling coordination within the agreed site scope.
  • Stock receipt, issue, consumption and reorder tracking across HVAC, electrical, plumbing and fire systems.
  • Coordination of task-appropriate tools, testing equipment, lifting equipment and PPE.
05

Service organisation

  • Site-specific facility leadership, operations management and discipline-level technical supervision.
  • Helpdesk/CMMS, EHS, energy monitoring, MIS and administration support.
  • Deployment coordination for HVAC operators, electricians, plumbers, STP operators, fire technicians, ELV/BMS technicians, helpers and storekeepers.
  • In-house and specialist-partner delivery models with agreed shifts, escalation routes and accountability.
MEASURABLE DELIVERY

Reports & deliverables

The reporting framework is agreed for your facility and may include:

  • Asset register: equipment identification, location, capacity and criticality.
  • PPM calendar: approved maintenance frequency for each maintainable asset.
  • Daily logs: operating status, readings, alarms and abnormalities.
  • Work-order register: complaint, priority, response time, actions and closure.
  • Breakdown reports: root cause, corrective action, downtime and recurrence.
  • Testing reports: HVAC, electrical, plumbing and fire-system test results.
  • Compliance register: applicable requirements and renewal dates.
  • Monthly FM report: PPM completion, breakdowns, downtime, utility use and pending issues.
  • Spares register: stock, consumption, reorder levels and outstanding procurement.

Scope & service coordination

Response and restoration times, 24/7 coverage, staffing, maintenance frequencies, tools, consumables, spares and testing responsibilities are agreed in the contract. Major replacements, upgrades, modifications, statutory fees and specialist work are priced separately where applicable.
