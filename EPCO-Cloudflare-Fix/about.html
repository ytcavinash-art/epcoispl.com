OUR DELIVERY MODEL

From requirement
to lifecycle support.

Six stages align engineering decisions, procurement, execution and operating needs to the same brief.

STAGE 01

Requirement & feasibility

Confirm the brief, site constraints, options and initial cost basis.

STAGE 02

Engineering & budgeting

Coordinate the design, BOQ, value engineering and approved budget.

STAGE 03

Procurement & mobilisation

Plan awards, long-lead orders, site establishment and labour deployment.

STAGE 04

Construction & controls

Execute against the programme, quality plan and cost reports.

STAGE 05

Testing & commissioning

Coordinate system tests, approval liaison, snag closure and documentation.

STAGE 06

Handover & operations

Prepare asset records, training, warranty management and maintenance mobilisation.
